Field notes

Buildings & roofs
The 98 building models use BAG footprints and 3DBAG LoD2.2 roof and wall surfaces. Roof colours and faint surface markings are sampled from the aerial photograph. Five small planes use the sample for their building. The original roof openings and courtyard boundaries are preserved. © 3DBAG by tudelft3d and 3DGI · CC BY 4.0 · PDOK aerial imagery
Ground & bridges
Roads, pavements, planted ground, canal banks and bridge footprints follow the Dutch base map. The BGT classifies the mapped carriageways, footways and parking strips here as open verharding (unit paving). Laser elevations supply the terrain and deck profiles. The terrain grid has 2 m spacing, with gaps filled from neighbouring samples. BGT base map · AHN elevation data
Trees
The register locates 43 distinct standing trees in the square. The 37 with a recorded height class are drawn at its midpoint. Six leaf symbols mark registered positions where the height class is unknown. Gemeente Amsterdam tree register
Street directions
Road centrelines, one-way tags, bicycle exceptions, canal routes and bridge numbers come from the 27 October 2026 map extract. Illustrated trips follow permitted directions and reserve space through turns. Walking routes follow BGT footways. © OpenStreetMap contributors · ODbL
Boats & vehicles
Rijkswaterstaat lists 6.8 m by 2.4 m navigable openings on the Reguliersgracht at the north and south quays. Long tour boats follow the Keizersgracht. The road models use Dutch yellow plates and light-blue taxi plates. Rijkswaterstaat waterways register · RDW plate colours
